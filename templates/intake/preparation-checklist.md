# Preparation checklist

Gather these before you fill the brief. None is required to start: whatever is missing becomes a question in the
interview. Synthetic or public material only; use role names, never people's names. Making samples and examples from
scratch takes about 30 to 40 minutes; reusing ones you already have is much quicker.

## Before you start

- [ ] I can say in two or three sentences what changes when this works.
- [ ] I know who uses the result and who approves it (roles).
- [ ] Nothing I plan to share is customer data, confidential, from my employer, or a credential.

## Put in the context pack

- [ ] **Good output examples:** at least two results you would accept as they are.
- [ ] **Bad output examples:** at least two results you would reject, each with the reason.
      (Longer examples go in `context/examples/`; they show what a result looks like and are never evidence. No answer
      keys: the factory writes its own test keys.)
- [ ] **Sample inputs:** two or three at real length (made up is fine).
- [ ] **Existing documents:** rules, templates or notes that describe how it is done today.
- [ ] **Glossary:** the terms you use that an outsider could misread.

## Decide or note

- [ ] **Who approves:** the role that approves at each gate.
- [ ] **What it may write to:** every system or file it changes, if any.
- [ ] **What is out of scope:** at least one thing it must not do.

## Ready to hand over

- [ ] Every file in the context pack is registered with its classification (synthetic or public).
- [ ] The brief's "you must answer" sections have an answer or "don't know yet".
