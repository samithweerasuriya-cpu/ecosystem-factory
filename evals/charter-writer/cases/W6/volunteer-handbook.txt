Riverside food bank volunteer handbook (synthetic). Section 3: shifts and swaps.

3.1 Shifts. The food bank runs four shifts a day on weekdays: sorting (08:00-11:00), packing (11:00-14:00), distribution (14:00-17:00) and the evening collection run (17:00-19:00). On Saturdays only sorting and distribution run.

3.2 Roles. Every shift needs a shift lead and at least three volunteers. Distribution needs at least one volunteer with the food hygiene certificate. The evening collection run needs a volunteer with a driving check on file.

3.3 Swaps. A volunteer who cannot do a shift should find a swap partner in the same role. Swaps must be asked for at least 72 hours before the shift starts. The coordinator approves swaps on the rota board.

3.4 Hygiene certificate. The food hygiene certificate is valid for three years. A volunteer whose certificate has run out may sort and pack, but not distribute.

3.5 Driving check. A driving check is valid for one year. Without one, a volunteer may ride along on the collection run but may not drive.

3.6 Sickness. A volunteer who is ill on the day phones the shift lead; that is not a swap and is never refused.

3.7 New volunteers. A new volunteer shadows three shifts before they count towards the minimum of three.

3.8 Records. The rota board in the warehouse office is the record of who works which shift.

3.9 Shift leads. A shift lead opens the warehouse, checks the team against the rota board and signs the shift sheet at the end. If a shift lead cannot come, the coordinator finds another lead; volunteers do not arrange this among themselves.

3.10 Sorting. Sorting volunteers check the dates on donated food, set aside anything past its date and put the rest on the shelves by type. Sorting needs no certificate.

3.11 Packing. Packing volunteers fill parcels from the parcel list for the day. A parcel for a family with a food allergy is packed separately and labelled by the shift lead.

3.12 Distribution. Distribution volunteers hand out parcels at the front door, check each family's referral slip and note the parcel number. At least one volunteer on the shift must hold the food hygiene certificate (see 3.4), because opened chilled food is sometimes handed out.

3.13 The collection run. The van leaves at 17:00 and visits the three supermarkets on the donation list. The driver checks the van before setting off and fills in the mileage book. A second volunteer rides along to load.

3.14 Public holidays. On public holidays only distribution runs, from 10:00 to 13:00. Swaps for a holiday shift follow the same rules as any other swap.

3.15 Lost property and keys. Keys to the warehouse are held by the shift leads and the coordinator. Lost property goes in the box in the office and is kept for a month.

3.16 Questions. Questions about this section go to the volunteer coordinator. The handbook is reviewed once a year by the trustees.
